# Publishing Your Documentation

## Publishing to the Web

1. Open your GitBook Space
2. Click **Share** (top-right)
3. Toggle **"Publish to web"** ON
4. GitBook assigns a free URL: `https://yourname.gitbook.io/your-space`

## Visibility Settings

| Setting | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Public | Anyone can find and view it |
| Unlisted | Accessible via link, not indexed |
| Private | Only invited members can view |

Choose **Public** for this assignment.

## Custom Domains (Optional)

Free plans support the default `gitbook.io` subdomain. 
Custom domains require a paid plan.

## Sharing Your URL

Once published, copy the URL from the **Share** panel and submit it.
Your documentation is now live and accessible to anyone!
